## Evidence boundaries

Original version 1.0.0 was developed against a private belt/strap example. Public tests and the example generator create synthetic geometry. The private source, exported production meshes, screenshots of that asset, absolute job paths and font/dependency binaries are not distributed. Historical counts are identified as historical in evidence/public_validation.json. Lower-reasoning model evaluation remains unexecuted.
