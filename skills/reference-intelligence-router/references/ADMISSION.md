# Knowledge Admission and Promotion

## States

DISCOVERED: a source or method was found. No operational authority.
SOURCE_VERIFIED: the exact source was opened and materially relevant claims were checked.
METHOD_EXTRACTED: a reusable principle/procedure was separated from project-specific assets and unsupported claims.
TESTED: the method was used on a bounded representative task and observable output was reviewed.
ADOPTED: the method improved quality/reliability or reduced burden enough to become a reusable default/candidate.
WATCH: promising, but evidence is incomplete, stale, conflicting or too narrow.
REJECTED: do not use as a positive method/reference unless the purpose is failure analysis.
SUPERSEDED: historical evidence only; a newer rule/source controls.

## Promotion record
Store ID, source/version/commit, task family, medium/channel, reusable method, non-transferable assets, known failure modes, authority/rights, freshness trigger, retrieval tags, and eval evidence.

## Evidence rule
A source can teach a method without proving the method is universally superior.
Do not promote popularity as quality, one aesthetic preference as universal, a successful output without isolating why it worked, vendor marketing as independent performance evidence, or model self-scores as acceptance.

## Failure promotion
A severe material failure may create an immediate prevention rule when the cause is clear and the rule is bounded.
Taste feedback usually needs task/medium scoping. Never turn one owner rejection into a universal aesthetic ban.
