# Human Pilot Feedback — Quality vs Usage — 2026-09-29

Source: owner-reported feedback from two external testers.

## Feedback signal

Tester feedback converged on the same product trade-off.

Positive:
- found overlapping genres/categories the tester had not known;
- brought representative games/products for each overlapping element and compared them;
- searched very thoroughly for products with a similar feel;
- usage visibly decreased in the optimized build.

Negative:
- the reduced-usage version felt less rich/detailed than the earlier version;
- perceived difference from asking ordinary ChatGPT was not large enough;
- the loss of richness was described as a feeling, not a measured quality score.

Owner decision:
- maximize result quality/performance even if usage rises;
- retain efficiency only for pure duplication/waste.

## Claim ceiling

This is qualitative pilot feedback, not a controlled benchmark.
It does not establish willingness to pay, market-wide preference, quantified quality gain, or exact
token-cost relationships.

## Product implication

Protect high-recall overlap/reference discovery as a signature behavior.
Do not trade away material discovery/detail to optimize usage.
External blind comparison against general AI remains required before commercial superiority claims.
