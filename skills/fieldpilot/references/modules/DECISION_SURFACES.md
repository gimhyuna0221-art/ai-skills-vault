# Decision-material surfaces for an already-built product

Use with DECISION_CONTINUITY, not as a second report. Contents: scope; evidence;
alternatives/economics; risk/change; experiment; pattern/handoff; card; delta/variants.

## Scope and progressive delivery

Load for broad existing-product commercialization, or a specific decision needing
one of these surfaces. A narrow price/fact answer uses the kernel's direct source
and material billing/date limits only; do not load or print this schema for it.
Choose surfaces by whether they can change the decision, not keyword counts.
Preserve the six-field first layer and nine-field buyer meanings in the ordinary
module. These are semantic additions inside the same answer, not twenty headings.
For broad decisions, explicitly cover reality, alternatives/economics, decisive
risks, experiment or its HOLD reason, and coverage. Conditional surfaces below
must appear only when relevant. In Route B, integrate them into the existing
professional report and its source/raw appendix rather than replacing that route.

## REALITY_CHECK / EVIDENCE_TRACE / PAIN_SIGNAL_SYNTHESIS

REALITY_CHECK connects observed pain/demand signals, competing ways to solve the
job, timing and the burden of changing behavior to this product's current state.
Distinguish crowded alternatives from demonstrated saturation; neither number of
competitors nor search-zero establishes demand, absence, or an attractive market.
Conditional sizing uses the existing sizing method: defined population, period,
units, sourced inputs, assumptions and sensitivity; missing inputs mean UNKNOWN,
never an invented TAM/SAM/SOM. No opaque viability or success-probability score.

For every material external claim maintain one reusable EVIDENCE_TRACE row:

`CLAIM_ID | claim | source locator/section | source class | source date/access date
| VERIFIED/INFERENCE/UNKNOWN | freshness/access status | counterevidence/limit`

VERIFIED is bounded to what was actually read: vendor copy can verify a listed
offer or vendor assertion, not its promised performance. A user-supplied excerpt
is SUPPLIED_EXCERPT, not independently live-verified. Record missing dates or
locators honestly. Inferences cite their premise IDs and remain inferences.
Make a second verification pass over decision-bearing claims: check entailment,
source independence, billing/time conditions, contradictions and product wiring.
Failed access cannot become support. Link IDs beside the claims in the answer;
keep full rows in a supporting trace/appendix with a real locator if saved. Never
say an appendix was saved or accessible unless it exists. Do not bury caveats.

Pain synthesis records unique authors/events after dedup, sampled source classes,
dates/window, observed recurrence and expressed frustration, plus contradictory
experience. Separate number of independent sampled reports from frequency per
person/time and market prevalence. Without denominators, frequency/prevalence is
UNKNOWN. A repost is not a second pain event; community complaints are not this
product's completed customer study. Triangulate accessible independent classes
when useful; a vendor summary is not an independent customer source. If only one
class or one anecdote is available, state LIMITED and its decision consequence.

## ALTERNATIVE_MAP / COMPETITOR_ECONOMICS / MONEY_SHAPE

Within COMPETITIVE_ALTERNATIVES, check direct, indirect, manual, service,
spreadsheet and do-nothing options for the buyer's job. Include each material
class, with source-supported named examples where available. Classes may overlap:
label overlaps rather than inventing six distinct competitors. If a class is not
material, say why briefly; absence of evidence is UNKNOWN, not non-applicability.
Distinguish observed usage from plausible substitutes. Preserve category aliases,
negative closure and CONFIRMED_OVERLAP / UNVERIFIED_OVERLAP / CONFIRMED_GAP /
UNKNOWN_GAP; a missing feature mention is still COMPETITOR_FEATURE_NOT_FOUND.

For decision-bearing alternatives, record commercial model, listed amount and
currency, billing period/upfront total, relevant tier/eligibility/tax/date, source,
and migration/setup/training/integration/data-loss friction. Pricing is not buyer
spend; plausible switching friction is INFERENCE until observed. UNKNOWN is valid.
Traction, retention or UNIT_ECONOMICS_SIGNAL appears only when decision-material
and supported by legitimate evidence: name cohort, metric definition, period,
denominator, costs included/excluded and provider/self-report limitations. Revenue
is not profit; funded capital is not sales; downloads are not paid retention.
Do not calculate LTV/CAC, margins or churn from absent/incompatible denominators.

MONEY_SHAPE connects user versus payer, observed current alternative spend or
UNKNOWN, a sourced pricing clue or labeled test-price hypothesis, switching burden,
and the current commercial rung. Keep LISTED_PRICE, ACTIVE_OFFER, PREORDER_OFFER,
OBSERVED_PURCHASE, OBSERVED_REPEAT_PURCHASE_OR_RETENTION and PROVEN_WTP distinct.
A paid deposit/preorder may establish that scoped transaction; record refundable
terms, fulfillment and cancellation status. One payment does not establish broad
WTP, repeat demand or PMF. Preserve actual product inspection and WHY_SWITCH_OR_NOT.

## TOP_KILLERS / CHANGE_OPTIONS

For a consequential broad judgment, select 1–3 decisive risks/unknowns: what could
invalidate this offer or next allocation, evidence ID or missing evidence, how the
failure would manifest, and one mitigation or falsifiable test. No generic risk
laundry list, probability score or feature-count ranking. If the evidence does
not support naming a risk as fact, call it an uncertainty.

On CHANGE/HOLD, offer 1–3 evidence-backed IMPROVE / PIVOT / SIMPLIFY directions
only where support exists. Each says which risk/claim it addresses, what stays,
what changes, which new assumption it introduces, and the smallest discriminator.
Do not invent three options to fill a quota; unsupported options stay UNKNOWN.
On BUILD/KEEP, omit unnecessary pivots. Rank/select one next action; optional
directions do not become multiple simultaneous homework assignments.

## VALIDATION_EXPERIMENT / FIRST_PAID_PROOF

Use the existing one next action, with: target and reachable exposure definition;
offer or behavior; input/deliverable; measured event and evidence capture; KPI
numerator/denominator; threshold; timebox; PASS / FAIL / HOLD rules. Distinguish a
proposed decision threshold from an observed metric or a validated benchmark.
Choose a falsifiable threshold proportionate to this decision; missing baseline
means proposed, not statistically proven. Name who prepares/executes it. FieldPilot
prepares accessible materials now; actual outreach, posting or charging remains
subject to the user's authorization. Skipping a test preserves the bounded answer.

For FIRST_PAID_PROOF the counted event must be actual payment, paid deposit or
paid preorder for the named offer, with amount/terms/payment locator/refunds where
known. Interest, waitlist, survey intent, clicks or an unpaid invoice never count.
PASS supports only the predeclared transaction criterion, not validated demand.
FAIL requires the declared exposure/time opportunity and absent threshold; failed
tracking, insufficient exposure, or unready delivery is HOLD, not disproven demand.
Refunded/cancelled transactions remain in history but do not count as retained
payment proof. If a paid offer is unsupported, state HOLD + exact prerequisite;
use an accessible non-paid diagnostic where appropriate, not a fabricated offer.

## REFERENCE_PATTERN / BUILD_HANDOFF_BRIEF

When a real product's onboarding/paywall/flow is decision-material, describe the
observed pattern, lawful locator/section/version, relevance, limits, and exact
build implication. Marketing text alone cannot establish unseen screen behavior.
Distinguish documented from personally observed flow; inaccessible content stays
UNKNOWN. Study the pattern, do not copy pixels, branding, wording or code. A flow
reference is not evidence of conversion lift, demand or revenue; no paid MCP dependency.

When implementation is selected, BUILD_HANDOFF_BRIEF wraps the existing exact
GOAL / IN_SCOPE / OUT_OF_SCOPE / WHY_NOW / MARKET_EVIDENCE_LINK / STILL_UNVERIFIED /
DONE_WHEN / DO_NOT_BUILD contract. Add inspected product revision/paths when known,
observable acceptance and how to verify; unknown paths must be inspected by the
receiving coder, never invented. An optional IMPLEMENTATION_PROMPT carries this
same bounded brief into Claude Code/Codex/Cursor, without new requirements or
authority to ship. FieldPilot itself does not edit code or start a coding loop.

## SHAREABLE_DECISION_CARD / CLAIM_CEILING / COVERAGE

Use a compact buyer-readable card as the first layer or on request: product and
candidate buyer; current decision; strongest evidence and counterevidence with
IDs/links; what to keep/change/hold; decisive unknowns; one next action with its
test criterion. Label hypotheses plainly; use the user's language. Reuse this as
the answer summary rather than duplicating it. A card must stand alone honestly.
Keep detailed trace/deep competitor teardown/raw excerpts in an actual separate
supporting section/artifact when needed; professional mode retains its raw appendix.
Do not force file creation for an ordinary short answer. Reusable context can be
the existing decision snapshot with source IDs, no persistent database required.

CLAIM_CEILING distinguishes VERIFIED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN per material finding;
COVERAGE carries actual source/facet OK / LIMITED / BLOCKED / NOT_CHECKED /
NOT_APPLICABLE plus effect on the decision. Coverage is never a completeness claim.
No superiority, sales, WTP, PMF, token savings or live-discovery quality claims
without observed evidence; public product marketing is not comparative quality proof.

## RETURNING_EVIDENCE_DELTA / COMPARE_VARIANTS

Preserve baseline ID/revision, decision, evidence/counterevidence IDs, product refs,
commercial rung and freshness. Emit WHAT_CHANGED / WHAT_STAYED_STABLE /
UPDATED_DECISION; attach new IDs to affected claims, retain old facts (including
payments later refunded), and refresh only affected/stale sources. Never silently
overwrite history or rerun unrelated sections. A missing baseline is stated.

COMPARE_VARIANTS is optional for two or more real product/change directions. Use
one frozen evidence baseline, same buyer/job/timeframe, common decision criteria,
and the same UNKNOWN/claim ceilings. Compare fit, switching burden, implementation
delta, decisive risks and smallest discriminator using shared source IDs. If new
evidence is needed for one variant, label that asymmetry and rebaseline all affected
rows before ranking. No synthetic market sizes, numeric winner score, or success
probabilities. A conditional choice or HOLD is valid when evidence cannot separate
variants. Keep one next action, not a parallel execution plan.
