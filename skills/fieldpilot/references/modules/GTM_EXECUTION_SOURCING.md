# v1.6 — GTM Execution Sourcing: DIY vs Specialist vs Agency vs Cofounder

## Purpose

This module answers a practical founder question that appears repeatedly after a product is built:

> "마케팅을 내가 해야 하나, 마케터를 구해야 하나, 외주/대행을 써야 하나, 공동창업자가 필요한가?"

This is a **resource-allocation and execution-design decision**, not evidence that one staffing model is universally superior. The framework below is FieldPilot-native decision support unless a specific labor/platform claim is separately sourced.

## First diagnose the bottleneck

```text
MARKET_UNCERTAINTY          who/what problem/value is still unclear
POSITIONING_OR_CHANNEL      target/message/route is unclear
SPECIALIST_EXECUTION        e.g. paid-media setup, lifecycle, SEO implementation
CAPACITY                    strategy is defined; founder simply cannot execute enough
ACCESS_OR_CREDIBILITY       a role/partner controls trusted access the founder lacks
LONG_TERM_GROWTH_OWNERSHIP  growth strategy is core, persistent and needs co-ownership
```

Do not treat `I hate marketing` or `I have zero users` as a diagnosis by itself.

## Route candidates

### Founder-led / DIY learning
Plausible when the decisive uncertainty is still about target, problem, message, channel, or offer. The goal is not to make the founder a career marketer; it is to prevent paying someone else to execute an undefined hypothesis.

Output must define the smallest learning action and artifact. Do not say "learn marketing first."

### Contractor / freelancer / fractional specialist
Plausible when a bounded specialist task can be specified and evaluated while the founder retains strategic learning and account/data ownership.

Examples of task shapes (not universal recommendations): analytics instrumentation, a scoped paid-search setup, lifecycle-email implementation, technical SEO audit, creative production.

### Agency
Plausible when the work requires recurring multi-skill execution or scale and the user has enough budget, measurement, offer/channel clarity, and management capacity to evaluate the work.

Do **not** assume an agency can manufacture product demand or identify PMF from an uninstrumented funnel.

### Marketing/growth employee
Plausible when there is sustained recurring work, organizational need, and enough runway for a role rather than a one-off task. Distinguish hiring a role from hiring a magic answer to an unresolved strategy question.

### Cofounder
Plausible only when the missing capability requires durable strategic ownership, shared company risk, repeated judgment, and a relationship worthy of equity/governance—not merely because the founder dislikes promotion.

Do not recommend giving equity to solve a short-term execution bottleneck.

### Distribution/access partner
Plausible when the scarce asset is trusted access, installed audience, channel rights, domain credibility, or complementary workflow rather than generic marketing labor.

## Paid-value execution brief

If external help is recommended, FieldPilot should produce a copy-ready brief containing:

```text
ROLE_OR_VENDOR_TYPE
PROBLEM_TO_SOLVE
WHAT_IS_ALREADY_KNOWN
WHAT_IS_NOT_THEIR_JOB_TO_INVENT
DELIVERABLES
ACCESS_NEEDED
METRICS / ACCEPTANCE LOGIC
ACCOUNT / PIXEL / ANALYTICS OWNERSHIP
REPORTING_REQUIREMENTS
TRIAL_BOUNDARY
STOP / REVIEW CONDITIONS
```

Numbers in a trial boundary must come from the user's budget/runway/test design or be labelled as a context-derived cap; never invent a universal project length or fee.

## Failure guards

- zero users -> automatically hire marketer;
- no PMF -> agency can find it for you;
- founder dislikes content -> must get a marketing cofounder;
- contractor executed a campaign -> channel is validated;
- agency case study -> same result is replicable;
- equity is cheaper than paying cash -> cofounder recommendation;
- external operator controls ad accounts, analytics, domains or customer data without an explicit ownership rationale;
- outsourcing strategy uncertainty while the founder loses the learning needed for future decisions.

## User-facing recommendation style

Prefer:

> "지금 병목은 마케팅 인력이 아니라 **누가 왜 살지 아직 불명확한 것**입니다. 그래서 당장 공동창업자를 구하기보다 당신이 직접 한 번의 타깃 인터뷰/오퍼 테스트를 돌리는 편을 권합니다. 그 결과 채널과 메시지가 반복 가능하게 잡히고 실행량이 병목이 되면 그때 specialist를 붙이는 것이 더 가역적입니다."

Or, when capacity is the real bottleneck:

> "전략과 측정은 이미 잡혀 있고 반복 작업량이 병목이므로, 현재는 공동창업자보다 **범위가 명확한 specialist trial**이 더 적합합니다. 아래 brief로 맡기고 계정·데이터는 당신이 소유하세요."
