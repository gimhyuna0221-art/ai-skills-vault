# v1.6 — Product-Type GTM Adapters

Adapters generate **candidate routes**. They do not hard-code one universal funnel.

## INDIE_STEAM_GAME

First questions:
- Is the concept/store promise understandable and attractive to the relevant player segment?
- Does a playable demo/Playtest produce meaningful play behavior and return/wishlist/feedback signals?
- What does official Steam visibility/traffic data show?

Candidate routes: Steam-native store/demo/Playtest/events, creator coverage, genre communities, external content.

Must not: treat wishlist count as product demand or a universal sales conversion formula.

## MOBILE_GAME

First questions:
- Does creative promise translate to install?
- Does first-session core loop produce activation and return?
- Can monetization plausibly support acquisition for the actual model/geography?

Candidate routes: store organic, short-form creative tests, platform ads when measurement/economics justify them.

Must not: install volume = game value.

## INTERACTIVE_WEB_CONTENT

First questions:
- Can a user understand and start immediately?
- Is completion/share/revisit behavior present?
- Is distribution search-led, discovery-led, embedded, or community-led?

Candidate routes: social, communities, SEO/search, embeds/partnerships.

Must not: views = meaningful engagement.

## B2C_MOBILE_APP

First questions:
- Is demand active-search or discovery-led?
- Does store/landing promise match first-session value?
- Do users return at the product's natural usage frequency?

Candidate routes: store optimization, SEO/search, short-form/demo content, creators, paid acquisition when justified.

Must not: store conversion = retention.

## AI_WEB_TOOL

First questions:
- Is the underlying job valuable independent of the "AI" label?
- Does output quality produce repeat task completion?
- Are API/marginal costs compatible with monetization?

Candidate routes depend on audience: search, niche communities, direct outreach, demos, creators, launch communities.

Must not: Product Hunt/Hacker News as universal default; AI category growth = product value.

## SUBSCRIPTION_SAAS

First questions:
- Is the pain recurring?
- Does activation lead to repeated value and renewal?
- Does acquisition fit the price/sales model?

Candidate routes: search/SEO/content, direct/partner sales, paid channels when unit economics and tracking are interpretable.

Must not: signup = PMF.

## B2B_SAAS

First questions:
- Who is user, decision-maker, payer?
- Is the ICP identifiable and reachable?
- Can product value be expressed in workflow/ROI terms?

Candidate routes: direct outreach, LinkedIn, search/content, partners, niche professional communities.

Must not: consumer funnel assumptions; Reddit/LinkedIn as mandatory defaults.

## CREATOR_UTILITY

First questions:
- Does the tool improve a repeated creator workflow?
- Is the output demonstrable/shareable?
- Are relevant creators reachable without depending on celebrity endorsement?

Candidate routes: creator outreach, tutorial/demo content, niche communities, search.

Must not: one famous creator's endorsement = reproducible GTM.

## MARKETPLACE

First questions:
- What exact transaction/liquidity must exist for users to receive value?
- Which side is the binding constraint now?
- Can a narrow segment/geography create useful liquidity?

Candidate routes are side-specific and context-dependent.

Must not: one-side-first as universal law; signups on one side = marketplace success.

## FREE_AD_SUPPORTED

First questions:
- Is there repeated use/session inventory?
- Is the ad model technically and behaviorally feasible?
- Does paid acquisition have any plausible path to positive economics for the actual geo/format?

Candidate routes: organic/discovery/search/platform; paid acquisition is allowed when product-specific economics justify it.

Must not: universal paid-acquisition ban; traffic alone = viable business.

## Cross-adapter build-method guard

`AI-built`, `vibe-coded`, `no-code`, or traditionally coded are implementation attributes, not direct market-demand evidence.

Only let build method affect GTM/market advice through observable consequences such as:
- reliability and defect rate;
- security/privacy posture;
- maintainability and support capacity;
- performance/cost;
- regulatory compliance;
- buyer trust or procurement requirements.

If users merely express stigma toward AI-built software, treat that as a testable trust/positioning hypothesis. Do not infer demand failure without product-specific behavior.
