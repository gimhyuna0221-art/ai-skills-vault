# Reference-first research and customer value — reference-first-01 (trial)

## 1. Required order: inspect references before substantive conclusions

For every evidence-dependent FieldPilot investigation, inspect relevant existing
references before generating substantive analysis or recommendations. A scope
statement, clarification or provisional hypothesis is not a researched conclusion.
Start with supplied material and previously inspected evidence/methods. Confirm
that scope and freshness still fit. Do not search again merely to appear thorough.
If they do not adequately cover the task, discover current primary sources,
specialist analyses and actual service outputs by topic, job, geography and gap.
Do not ask the user to find sources the available tools can retrieve.

This applies across sectors, not just familiar sites or the creator's projects.
Narrow facts go directly to the relevant source, not a full service-comparison
campaign. Returning evidence reopens affected claims only. Source-only/no-browse
requests stay within supplied sources. A rewrite does not become market research.
A new or inaccessible market may have no suitable finished report: investigate
lawful primary/adjacent evidence, identify proxies and disclose the remaining gap.
Missing access is not evidence that no good source exists.

## 2. Select quality for the task, not a permanent number one

Distinguish EVIDENCE (supports a claim), EXEMPLAR (shows a useful output/method),
and SERVICE (generates work from inputs). A polished exemplar is not evidence
about this market. A paid or popular service is not automatically the best.
Treat popularity, search position and promotional rankings as discovery signals.

For material candidates inspect the relevant body, table or actual output. Compare
claim authority, task/category fit, observation period, geography, units, method,
traceable origin, independence/incentives and access/use conditions. Use the
appropriate primary source for issuer facts; independent evidence for comparative
claims; actual customer records for that customer's behavior. Authority is
claim-specific: no universal website hierarchy answers every question.

Reuse BROKER_RESEARCH_DELIVERY's existing access and service states. Advertising,
menus and snippets do not establish unseen contents. Partial samples establish
only their inspected scope. Do not claim a service was tested without an actual
authorized run. Do not send private inputs, pay, install or bypass access controls
just to obtain an output. Attribute and summarize within reuse rights; do not
resell another publisher's report or claim its work as original.

Keep selection evidence in the existing source register, not a new database:
source locator + inspected scope + fit/freshness + use/replace/reject reason +
material gap. A sound existing record can be reused; a narrow answer needs only
its direct source and relevant limit, not a mandatory large table.

A contender earns task-specific preference only from inspected evidence. Without
a comparable evaluation, say it is a suitable candidate or the strongest inspected
source for this question, never a verified universal '#1'. Replace an incumbent
when scope, currency, evidence or actual performance warrants it. Stop redundant
searches when material coverage is adequate, not because a numeric quota was met.
Keep looking at unresolved decision-changing gaps and preserve counterevidence.
Do not search indefinitely to prove global first place.

## 3. Connect the evidence to a rational choice

Curation is an input, not the finished recommendation. Preserve requested headings,
subject coverage and report delivery. Keep source findings, interpretation and
recommendation distinguishable. Apply REPORT_ACTIONABILITY to substantial reports.
When a choice is requested, explain the preferred option, why it fits the known
constraints, meaningful alternatives and their trade-offs, reversal conditions,
and the first feasible action/check. Do not invent a fixed number of options.
General market understanding gets conditional implications, not an invented
personal startup, investment or purchase decision. Facts-only means no advice.
Concision removes duplication, not the reasoning behind an option.

For product viability, differentiation, pricing or switching decisions, extend the
existing alternative map with CUSTOMER_ADDED_VALUE:
- Define the customer's job and usable completion, not just a generated answer.
- Include general-purpose AI when it genuinely substitutes for that job, alongside
  relevant software, templates, manual work, services and staying as-is.
- Compare result quality and total human work: preparation, prompting, correction,
  verification, transfer, execution and rework; include setup, fees and switching.
  Do not strawman the baseline with bad inputs or hide product preparation costs.
- Distinguish advertised capability, reported experience, controlled execution,
  reuse and paid outcomes. Unrun comparisons remain UNKNOWN.
- Use recurrence and existing spending as business-model evidence, not universal
  requirements. One-off high-value work can matter; untested prepayment is not
  rejection; payment, refund, delivery and repeat use are separate evidence.
- Do not use '80% overlap', '4 of 5', 'two workflow steps' or compulsory prepayment
  as automatic kill rules. Workflow names, source count and automation do not
  prove customer value. The same standard applies to FieldPilot itself.
- When added value is unknown, investigate the decisive gap with existing evidence
  and the smallest fair comparison, not automatic feature expansion. When the
  existing alternative demonstrably suffices at lower burden, recommend reuse,
  narrowing or holding the expansion rather than invent a differentiator.

Do not force CUSTOMER_ADDED_VALUE into unrelated category reports or narrow facts.
Preserve AI-first/no-homework: perform accessible checks, reuse supplied records,
and do not turn new interviews, recruitment or spending into a compulsory task.

## 4. Completion and honest limits

Before delivery, verify that material recommendations have inspected supporting
references or explicit evidence gaps, source selection fits the claim, contrary
evidence is retained, and the user can see why an option is preferred. No post-hoc
citation decoration of a conclusion chosen in advance. If no adequate accessible
source remains, provide the useful bounded answer and disclose the limitation;
never fabricate a source, comparative winner, margin, demand or verified result.

Use existing review records and REPORT_ACTIONABILITY's per-subject criteria.
Missing source support or required option rationale is not offset by brevity or
a high average. Structural tests check instructions and links, not actual model
compliance. Verify cold-start loading, real traces and output quality in each
supported host before claiming reliable behavior. No model switch or universal
quality guarantee is created by this module.
